\section{\textbf{Slightly longer story}}
\vspace{1mm}
\small{My research lies at the intersection of robotics and perception, with a focus on enhancing autonomous underwater systems. I work within a perception lab to bring advanced perceptual capabilities to robots, enabling them to perform complex tasks in dynamic oceanic environments. I view robotics and perception as deeply interwoven: robots need to move to perceive their surroundings effectively, and active perception, in turn, aids them in executing tasks. Specifically, I develop frameworks for autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs) to detect and map marine objects like oyster beds and coral reefs using both real and synthetic data. I leverage simulation-based techniques, data-driven decision-making, and multi-modal sensor integration to create robust systems that thrive in challenging conditions. My ultimate goal is to advance autonomous systems that support conservation, research, and sustainable marine ecosystem management.}
% \small{My research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning, computer vision, and robotics, specifically focused on enhancing autonomous systems for underwater exploration and environmental monitoring. This work involves developing advanced perception and navigation frameworks for autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs) to efficiently detect and map marine objects, such as oyster beds and coral reefs, using both real and synthetic data. I am particularly interested in leveraging simulation-based techniques, data-driven decision-making, and multi-modal sensor integration to create robust robotic systems that can operate effectively in challenging oceanic environments. My ultimate goal is to advance autonomous systems that can support conservation, research, and sustainable management of marine ecosystems.}
