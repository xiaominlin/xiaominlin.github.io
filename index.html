🔥 About Me

I am a Tenure-Track Assistant Professor of Electrical Engineering at the University of South Florida, with an affiliated appointment in the Bellini College of Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity and Computing (Computer Science) and a researcher appointment in the Institute for Artificial Intelligence + X. My work spans edge AI, perception-enabled robotics, and intelligent systems for challenging environments, including underwater, agricultural, and healthcare domains.

I direct the ERA Lab (Embodied Robotics and Autonomy), which focuses on robotics, multimodal perception, and AI for challenging environments.

🌱🌟 I am actively recruiting PhD students to join my lab starting Spring 2027 and Fall 2027. I also welcome collaboration in robotics, AI, edge computing, and medical or underwater applications.

📬 If you are interested, please fill out the form after reading my Research Statement pdf. Ideally, I am looking for experienced students with a top-tier publication and an MS degree in related fields, and a strong background in robotics, AI, or embedded systems. Only if you are at USF, feel free to email me with your CV, transcripts, and a brief statement of research interests and career goals. Unfortunately, I cannot respond to all inquiries, especially those without sufficient information.

I received my PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Maryland, advised by Prof. Yiannis Aloimonos. My research lies at the intersection of robotics and perception, with emphasis on autonomous underwater systems. I build frameworks for autonomous underwater vehicles to detect and map marine objects such as oyster beds and coral reefs using real and synthetic data, simulation-based methods, and multimodal sensing.

Before joining USF, I was a postdoctoral researcher with the Energy Efficient High Performance Computing Lab (EEHPC) at Johns Hopkins University, directed by Dr. Tinoosh Mohsenin, where I was also an Institute for Assured Autonomy Postdoctoral Fellow. There I expanded my focus to integrating large language models with knowledge graphs for advanced reasoning and decision making, and to building efficient algorithms and frameworks for deployment on edge devices and robots.
